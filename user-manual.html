User Manual

KOReader
Manager Suite

Save web articles from your browser and read them on your e-ink device — no fuss.

This tool is a three-step pipeline. Each step hands off to the next. You only need to do the installation once — after that, the routine takes a couple of minutes.

① Select & Export › ② Convert to EPUB › ③ Send to Kindle
0 Once

Install the Chrome Extension

One-time setup — takes about two minutes.

🔧 Chrome, Edge, or Brave

  • Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter.
  • Flip the Developer mode toggle (top-right corner) to ON.
  • Click Load unpacked and select the KOReader Manager folder you downloaded.
  • The extension icon appears in your toolbar. Done!

🦊 Firefox

  • Type about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox in the address bar.
  • Click Load Temporary Add-on and open the manifest.json inside the extension folder.
📝

Firefox note: The extension resets when you close the browser. For everyday use, Chrome/Edge is recommended.

📌

Pin it: Click the puzzle-piece 🧩 icon in Chrome's toolbar and hit Pin next to KOReader Manager so it's always one click away.

1 Step

Select & Export Your Articles

Pick which bookmarks you want to read on your Kindle.

The extension shows all your browser bookmarks as a tidy, clickable list — including folders. You tick what you want, then export.

1
Open the extension

Click the KOReader Manager icon in your toolbar. The Bookmarks tab opens by default, showing all your bookmark folders.

2
Select what you want to read

Tick individual article links, or click a folder name to select everything inside it at once. You can also hit Send Current Tab to instantly add the page you're currently viewing.

3
Click "Export Selected"

A file called urls.csv is saved automatically to your Downloads folder. That's the handoff to the next step.

💡

You can run this as often as you like throughout the week, adding new articles as you find them. The converter in Step 2 will pick them all up at once.

2 Step

Convert Articles to EPUB

Turn those URLs into real book files — automatically.

The converter program reads the urls.csv file, downloads each article (including images), and turns them into EPUB files ready for your Kindle.

1
Double-click "Article to Ebook Converter.exe"

That's the only thing you need to do. It finds the urls.csv automatically.

🛡️

Windows security warning? Click More info → Run anyway. This is normal for programs not from the Microsoft Store.

2
Watch the progress

A small window shows each article being processed. A ✅ means success; a ❌ means that site couldn't be downloaded (paywalls, for example — other articles still convert fine).

3
Your EPUBs are ready

When finished, the tool opens the output folder automatically — it's Downloads/EPUBS. The urls.csv file is deleted to keep things tidy.

3 Step

Send Files to Your Kindle

Copy your books wirelessly — no cables needed.

The extension's second tab, Kindle Finder, locates your Kindle on the Wi-Fi network and opens its file browser right in Chrome, so you can drag files straight in.

1
Prepare your Kindle

On your Kindle, open KOReader and launch the File Browser. Your computer and Kindle must both be on the same Wi-Fi network.

2
Scan for your Kindle

Click the extension icon, go to the Kindle Finder tab, and hit Scan Network. The extension searches your local network automatically.

⚠️

Can't find it? Check KOReader → Settings → Network → Network Info → IP and type that address manually into the extension's IP field.

3
Drag and drop your EPUBs

Once found, click Open Files. Your Kindle's folder appears in the browser — drag the EPUB files from the Downloads/EPUBS folder straight into it.

4
Open KOReader and read!

The books appear in KOReader's library immediately. Next time, skip the scan — just click Open Files directly.

🔌

Prefer a cable? Connect your Kindle via USB, open its drive, and copy EPUBs into files/mnt/us/koreader/HOME/Books. Then open KOReader to see them.
